## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-energy, international shopping and curation platform that bridges youthful spontaneity with elite execution. The aesthetic marries high-contrast, luminous color play with rigorous contemporary editorial discipline. It avoids clinical coldness in favor of a warm, radiant, and optimistic luxury posture. 

The aesthetic philosophy draws on contemporary layered neo-digital design: generous curved surfaces, layered tinted planes, fluid asymmetric product cards, and rhythmic color blocking. The interface feels tactile yet ultra-modern, inviting discovery through bold contrast, radiant micro-interactions, and expansive, warm canvases that celebrate tactile commerce.

## Colors

The palette balances the fiery dynamism of Vibrant Orange with the regal prestige of Deep Purple, anchored against an ink-rich Deep Navy rather than flat monochrome grays.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#FF6B35`)**: Exclusively drives commerce mechanics—primary conversion buttons, deal badges, cart counts, critical forward navigation, and checkout confirmation.
- **Secondary (`#5B3FD1`)**: Signals curation prestige, verified vendor badges, member-exclusive banners, and interactive navigational chrome.
- **Tertiary (`#3B82F6`)**: Powers logistical clarity, tracking markers, operational alerts, and secondary utility toggles.
- **Neutral (`#111827`)**: Governs high-clarity typography, stark structural dividers, and grounded navigational bottom sheets.
- **Atmospheric Backgrounds**: Instead of pure cool white, default surfaces employ `#FFFDF9` with sectional contextual washes (`#FFF4E8`, `#F3F0FF`, `#EFF6FF`) to create distinct thematic shopping realms without structural visual clutter.

## Typography

Typography pairs the structural warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the technical clarity of **Space Grotesk**. 

Headlines command visual presence through ultra-bold, tightly-tracked geometric letterforms, creating immediate visual momentum. Body copy utilizes balanced weights with generous line heights to preserve ease of scanning across varied global merchandise descriptions. 

All metadata, price tags, currency indicators, SKU counts, and category tags shift intentionally to **Space Grotesk**, imbuing product specifications with a crisp, modern global trade precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a fluid 12-column dynamic grid on desktop, scaling down to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column framework on mobile. Spacing operates strictly off an 8-point base grid (with a 4px half-step for dense components).

### Spatial Hierarchy & Rhythm
- **Asymmetric Grid Modules**: Product feeds reject uniform listing cards in favor of dynamic 2-column or 3-column masonry clusters where featured items span two units horizontally or vertically, introducing curated rhythm.
- **Section Transitions**: Content blocks alternate between warm canvas whites and soft tinted washes (`#FFF4E8`, `#F3F0FF`), framed by full-width bleed containers with generous vertical padding (`space-2xl` on mobile, `space-3xl` on desktop).
- **Edge Restraint**: Content maintains robust safe margins to preserve high-end framing, avoiding screen-edge crowding while allowing horizontally scrollable preview tracks to peek cleanly from the viewport edge.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered, tinted ambient shadows and semi-translucent surface overlays, avoiding harsh drop shadows.

- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas)**: `#FFFDF9` warm base surface.
- **Surface Level 1 (Resting Cards)**: Crisp pure `#FFFFFF` resting on Level 0, framed by an ultra-subtle border (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05)`) and supported by a multi-tier ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(91, 63, 209, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Elements & Hover States)**: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(255, 107, 53, 0.12), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05)`, used for active cart drawers, elevated quick-view modals, and hovered product cards.
- **Surface Level 3 (System Overlays & Navigation Sheets)**: Translucent white overlays (`rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.88)`) backed by a `backdrop-blur(16px)` layer, trimmed with a hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`).
- **Chromatic Accent Depth**: Interactive floating purchase badges utilize a concentrated back-glow matched to the primary hue: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(255, 107, 53, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a sculptural, confident curvature (`roundedness: 2`). Standard structural surfaces, cards, and input fields utilize `0.5rem` to `1rem` corner rounding, while hero promotional enclosures, interactive chips, floating indicators, and quick-add controls scale up to `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`) and full pill radii (`9999px`). 

Angular corners are strictly avoided; contours should feel smooth, welcoming, and ergonomically tactile, reinforcing the playful yet luxurious brand identity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid `#FF6B35` fill, `#FFFFFF` bold typography, pill-shaped (`9999px`). Includes a warm diffuse shadow (`rgba(255, 107, 53, 0.3)`). Hover triggers a subtle upward lift (`-2px`) and an intensified glow.
- **Secondary Button**: Tinted container with `#F3F0FF` background, `#5B3FD1` text, and zero border. Hover shifts background to a deeper lavender tone.
- **Outlined / Tertiary Button**: Transparent fill, 1.5px solid `#111827` border, `rounded-lg` or pill shape, crisp high-contrast text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Chips**: Pill-shaped containers in neutral tinted tones (`#FFF4E8`, `#EFF6FF`). Active state swaps to solid `#111827` background with pure white text.
- **Status & Deal Badges**: Rendered using `Space Grotesk` uppercase text (`label-sm`), featuring high-contrast tinted pairings (e.g., `#ECFDF5` background with `#10B981` text for stock confirmations; `#FFF4E8` with `#FF6B35` for hot deals).

### Product Cards
- Encased in pure white with `rounded-xl` borders (16px to 24px).
- Product imagery floats against neutral tinted backdrops (`#FFFDF9`) without harsh internal dividing lines.
- Pricing displays utilize `Space Grotesk` with the integer scaled up and the currency mark raised.
- Includes a floating circular quick-add icon button resting in the bottom-right corner of the image plane.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Search bars utilize generous heights (48px to 56px) with fully rounded pill edges, warm off-white background fill (`#FFFDF9`), and an inset boundary of `1.5px solid rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08)`.
- Focused state transitions to a bold `1.5px solid #5B3FD1` with a soft purple outer ring spread (`0 0 0 4px rgba(91, 63, 209, 0.12)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkboxes utilize rounded squares (`rounded-md`, 6px), filling with `#5B3FD1` when selected and displaying a bold white checkmark.
- Radio buttons feature a clean concentric ring with smooth inner dot expansion in `#FF6B35`.

### Global Sourcing Indicators
- Contextual metadata components specific to this platform: a combined pill displaying a micro country flag, verified vendor icon, and currency conversion tooltip, styled with a subtle `#F3F0FF` background and `#5B3FD1` border highlights.
